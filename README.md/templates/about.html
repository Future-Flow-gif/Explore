About Us

Explore Bharat is a cultural tourism web application that digitally presents the rich and diverse heritage of India.

🌍 Our Vision

To promote cultural awareness by showcasing India’s traditions, festivals, art forms, cuisines, and heritage sites.

🎯 Our Purpose

To help users understand not only where to travel, but also why each destination is culturally significant.

📚 Who It’s For

Students, tourists, and cultural enthusiasts who want to explore and appreciate India’s living heritage.
